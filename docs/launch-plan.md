# Launch plan

> Türkçe: [launch-plan.tr.md](launch-plan.tr.md). This English text is the source of truth.

Most channels give a project one real chance at attention. Launch only when a
visitor can play a game within minutes, not while the repo is a connection test.

## Pitch

> Jackbox-style party games with no app and no internet, on a $5 ESP32.

## Ready before launch (roadmap phases 1–3)

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Playable Vampires & Villagers | People share the game, not the connection test |
| "Flash from browser" (ESP Web Tools on GitHub Pages) | Installing PlatformIO is the biggest barrier; plug in, click, play |
| 30-second video | 6–8 people at a café join with their phones, no app, no internet, the vampire kills someone at night. This shot sells it |
| GIF/video at the top of the README, then a 2-minute quick start | A visitor decides in seconds |
| Release `.bin`s and a social preview image | For people who will not build anything |
| "Add your own game" guide + `good first issue`s | Contributors adding games keep the project growing |

## Channels

| Channel | What to post | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hackaday (email tips@hackaday.com) | Short description, video, repo link | They often cover ESP32 projects; a write-up brings links from other sites |
| Hacker News — Show HN | "Show HN: Party games on a $5 ESP32, no app, no internet" | Tue–Thu, US morning; answer comments yourself for the first hours |
| Reddit | r/esp32, r/arduino (technical); r/boardgames (the game) | Read each subreddit's self-promotion rules first |
| Hackster.io, Instructables | Bill of materials, wiring, enclosure build | Home of the box edition; Instructables runs contests |
| Espressif | Tag @EspressifSystem on X/Mastodon | They share community projects |
| Awesome lists | PRs to lists such as `awesome-esp` | Small but steady traffic |
| TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts | Vertical cut of the café video | Reaches people who are not engineers |
| Turkey | LinkedIn post; Technopat and DonanımHaber forums; a Turkish Medium/dev.to article | The Turkish UI is an advantage here |

## Order

1. Finish phases 1 and 2, play a real game with friends and film it.
2. Finish phase 3: browser flasher, releases, README.
3. Same week: Hackaday tip, Show HN, Reddit, LinkedIn.
4. Answer first-week issues quickly; people decide to star and contribute that week.
5. Every new game: a new short video and a small follow-up post.
