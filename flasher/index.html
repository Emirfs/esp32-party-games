ESP32 Party Games

Jackbox-style party games with no app and no internet, on a $5 ESP32.

This browser cannot flash boards. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop computer; Safari, Firefox and phones do not support Web Serial.

Flashing only works on an https:// page. Open https://emirfs.github.io/esp32-party-games/.

Steps

  1. Plug an ESP32-S3 board (8 MB flash or more) into this computer with a USB data cable.
  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop.
  3. Click Install, pick the board's serial port and wait about a minute. No port? Hold the BOOT button while plugging the board in.
  4. Power the board (a phone or power bank works). On every phone join the Wi-Fi PartyGames-XXXX with password partygames and open http://192.168.4.1.
  5. Everyone makes a profile; the first to tap Become admin starts Vampires & Villagers.

Flash without a browser

Download esp32-party-games-<version>.bin from Releases and write it at offset 0:

esptool.py --chip esp32s3 write_flash 0x0 esp32-party-games-<version>.bin
